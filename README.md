# Goat Yoga

A quick browser prototype of an idle/incremental game with a physical layout. The goats themselves do the yoga.

Play it here: https://geekahedron.github.io/goat-yoga/

## How to play

- Goats generate **zen** even on bare grass. A yoga mat doubles their output.
- Auras have different shapes: hay and smoothie bars boost adjacent tiles, incense runs a whole row and column, wind chimes cross on the diagonals, gongs pulse in a ring (and sometimes send a zen wave), and a disco ball covers a large radius. Overlapping auras **multiply**.
- Two calm goats (Pygmy or Mountain) standing next to each other unlock a partner-pose bonus. A line of 3+ goats triggers a chain combo, and a bigger wave if they share a pose.
- Rowdy kids wander and bump neighbors. Nothing ever breaks — sloppy layouts just run slower.
- Tap a goat to hold a harder pose for a burst of zen.
- Select a shop item to preview its range and description before buying. Tap a tile to aim, then tap it again (or **Place here**) to confirm — the first tap never spends zen. Tap the selected item again, tap off the grid, or press Escape to deselect.
- **Drag** an item to move or swap it, or **tap it, then tap a destination**. A goat on a mat moves together. Moving is free. Store, sell, or **Pose** from the tray under the yard.
- The shop is a compact tray along the edge so the yard stays visible. Pull it open for upgrades and retreat options.
- Progress saves in this browser. Offline zen accrues when you come back (capped). After enough lifetime zen you can move the retreat for a permanent multiplier.

This is a prototype to test whether the core loop is fun on a phone. You can also open `index.html` directly in a browser.

A new prototype of the same loop, reframed as a human neighborhood yoga studio (Goat Yoga is a later unlock). Yogis run classes, earn XP, and train in short minigames. Play it here: https://geekahedron.github.io/goat-yoga/yoga-mogul/
